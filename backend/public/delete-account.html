Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. This page explains how to delete your Spool Master account and what data will be removed.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your account and all your data are permanently deleted from our servers as soon as you confirm.

How to Delete Your Account

From the Spool Master app:

  1. Open the Spool Master app
  2. Go to Profile (tap the profile icon)
  3. Tap Account Settings
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete My Account
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted

What Data Will Be Deleted

The following data associated with your account will be permanently deleted:

  • Account Information: Email, username, profile picture
  • Filament Data: All your scanned spools and inventory
  • Scan History: All NFC scan records
  • Consumption History and Shopping List: All recorded consumption and planned purchases
  • Storage Locations and Notes: All text you entered for your spools
  • User Preferences: Language, currency, units settings
  • Subscription Information: Premium subscription status
  • Sync Data: All cloud-synchronized data

Data Retention Timeline

  • Immediately: Your account and all associated data are deleted from our servers, your Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked, and you are logged out
  • Within 30 days: Residual copies in server backups are overwritten
  • Exception: Subscription transaction records may be kept without any link to your account where required for accounting

Important Notes

  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription purchased through the App Store or Google Play: cancel it in your store account settings to stop future payments
  • Data stored locally on your device (offline cache) must be deleted manually by uninstalling the app
  • NFC tag data written to physical tags is not affected by account deletion

Can't Access the App?

If you can't access the app to delete your account, you can request deletion by email:

Email us at: privacy@engageengine.ch

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your account
  • Subject line: "Account Deletion Request - Spool Master"

We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, please contact us at privacy@engageengine.ch.
