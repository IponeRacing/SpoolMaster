Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 12, 2026

Welcome to Marqo. We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Marqo Camera and Marqo Prompteur.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email: Collected during Google or Apple Sign-In
  • Username: Provided during registration
  • Profile Picture: Optional via authentication provider
  • User ID: Automatically generated

1.2 Video & Content Data

  • Video Shots: Clips recorded in Marqo Camera and uploaded to our servers for processing and editing workflows
  • Media Metadata: Duration, resolution, timestamps, device information
  • Projects: Organized video marketing campaigns created by the user

1.3 Teleprompter Data (Marqo Prompteur)

  • Scripts: Text imported or created for teleprompter use
  • Edits: Modifications made to scripts
  • Voice Detection Data: Local analysis of speech to auto-scroll text (processed on device when possible)
  • Reading Progress: Paragraph or line tracking for synchronization

1.4 Usage Data

  • App Activity: Feature usage, session duration, navigation flow
  • Performance Data: Crash logs, diagnostics

1.5 Subscription Information

  • Purchase History: Subscription plans (monthly/annual)
  • Premium Status: Access level and entitlements

2. How We Use Your Data

2.1 Core Features

  • Provide video recording and upload services (Marqo Camera)
  • Enable server-side processing and editing workflows
  • Manage teleprompter scripts and playback (Marqo Prompteur)
  • Synchronize user projects across devices

2.2 Teleprompter Functionality

  • Display scripts in scrolling format for video recording
  • Auto-scroll based on voice detection (when enabled)
  • Support paragraph and line-by-line reading modes

2.3 Analytics & Improvement

  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Analyze usage patterns
  • Fix bugs and optimize workflows

3. Data Sharing with Third Parties

3.1 Services Used

  • Cloud Hosting Providers: Storage and processing of uploaded video content
  • Apple Sign-In: Authentication
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication
  • Apple StoreKit / Google Play Billing: Subscription management

3.2 We NEVER Sell Your Data

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Video content and scripts remain your property. Data is only shared with providers required for app functionality.

4. Your Rights

4.1 GDPR Rights (European users)

  • Right of Access: Request your data
  • Right of Rectification: Correct your data
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your account and content
  • Right to Object: Restrict processing
  • Right to Portability: Export your data

4.2 Consent Management

  • Tracking Consent: Managed via system settings
  • Voice Processing: Optional feature for teleprompter auto-scroll
  • Account Settings: Control data usage preferences

5. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

  1. Go to Profile
  2. Open Account Settings
  3. Select Delete My Account

All associated data (videos, scripts, projects) will be permanently removed after a retention period.

6. Data Security

  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transfers are encrypted
  • Secure Storage: Encrypted cloud storage for media and scripts
  • Access Control: Restricted server access
  • Authentication: Google and Apple Sign-In

7. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Stored while account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Removed within 30 days
  • System Logs: Retained up to 90 days
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained longer if required by law

8. Local Processing

Some features, such as voice detection for teleprompter auto-scrolling, may be processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers unless explicitly required.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will be communicated within the app or via email.

10. Third-Party Links

The app may include links to external services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Children's Privacy

Marqo is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors without parental consent.
