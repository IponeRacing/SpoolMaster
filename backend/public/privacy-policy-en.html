Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 12, 2026

Welcome to Spool Master. We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email: Collected during login via Google or Apple Sign-In
  • Username: Collected during login
  • Profile Picture: Optional, collected via Google/Apple Sign-In
  • User ID: Automatically generated by our system

1.2 Usage Data

  • Filament Spools: Information about your scanned spools (brand, color, weight, etc.)
  • Inventory: Management of your filament stock
  • Preferences: Language, currency, units, low stock threshold
  • NFC Data: NFC chip reads/writes (stored locally on chips)

1.3 Subscription Information

  • Purchase History: Spool Master subscriptions (monthly/annual)
  • Premium Status: Information about your active subscription

1.4 Advertising Data (non-premium users)

  • IDFA (iOS): Apple advertising identifier (if you accepted tracking)
  • Ad Interactions: Clicks, views, impressions
  • AdMob Data: Collected by Google AdMob to personalize ads

2. How We Use Your Data

2.1 App Functionality

  • Manage your account and profile
  • Store and synchronize your filament data
  • Manage your premium subscriptions
  • Provide technical support

2.2 Advertising (non-premium users only)

  • Display personalized ads via Google AdMob
  • Measure ad performance
  • Premium users see NO ads

2.3 Analytics and Improvement

  • Analyze app usage
  • Improve our features
  • Detect and fix bugs

3. Data Sharing with Third Parties

3.1 Services Used

  • Google AdMob: Advertising for non-premium users
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication
  • Apple Sign-In: Authentication
  • Apple StoreKit: Subscription management

3.2 We NEVER Sell Your Data

Your personal information is never sold to third parties. It is only shared with services necessary for app functionality, in accordance with their privacy policies.

4. Your Rights

4.1 GDPR Rights (European users)

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of your data
  • Right of Rectification: You can correct your data
  • Right to Erasure: You can delete your account and all your data
  • Right to Object: You can refuse ad tracking
  • Right to Portability: You can export your data

4.2 Consent Management

  • Ad Tracking (ATT): You can refuse in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking
  • GDPR: Consent form at first launch (EU users)
  • Premium Subscription: Removes all ads and tracking

5. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app:

  1. Go to Profile
  2. Click on Account Settings
  3. Select Delete My Account

Important: Account deletion is permanent and results in the deletion of all your data (spools, inventory, preferences, subscriptions). This action is irreversible.

6. Data Security

  • HTTPS Encryption: All communications are encrypted
  • Secure Storage: Passwords and tokens are encrypted
  • Secure Server: Hosted in Switzerland with high security
  • Strong Authentication: Via Google or Apple Sign-In

7. Data Retention

  • Active Account: As long as you use the app
  • Deleted Account: 30 days before permanent deletion
  • System Logs: Maximum 90 days
  • Subscription Data: Retained per legal obligations (7 years)

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The mobile app does not use cookies. Data is stored locally on your device via:

  • Secure Storage: For authentication tokens
  • Shared Preferences: For user preferences
  • Hive Database: For local data cache

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Important changes will be notified via the app or by email. The last update date is shown at the top of this page.

10. Third-Party Apps and Links

Our app may contain links to external websites (e.g., in ads). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites.

11. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors without parental consent.

12. Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: privacy@engageengine.ch
  • Website: https://api.engageengine.ch
